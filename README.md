# 🥥 Coconut Craze

Play it at https://verdeyen-k.github.io/coconutcraze/

Two players, red and blue, race to collect coconuts that fall from three palm trees (left, middle and right).
Walk over a coconut to pick it up, then carry it to your conveyor belt: red's is on the left, blue's is on the right.
Most points after 90 seconds wins.

| | Coconut | What it does on your belt |
|---|---|---|
| 🥥 | Coconut | 1 point for you |
| 🧊 | Ice coconut | Freezes the other player for 3 seconds |
| ➖ | Negative coconut | Takes 10 points from the other player (scores never go below 0) |

**Snatch!** Walk into the other player with empty hands to steal the coconut they're carrying.

## Controls

- **Keyboard:** arrow keys or W A S D. On one device, red uses W A S D and blue uses the arrow keys.
- **Phone or tablet:** push the joystick the way you want to walk. On one device, red's stick is on the left and blue's is on the right.

## Ways to play

- **vs Computer**
- **2 players, one device**
- **Online:** one person creates a room and the other types the 4-letter code on their own device.

Made with plain HTML and JavaScript. Online rooms use Firebase Realtime Database.

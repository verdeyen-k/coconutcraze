🥥 Coconut CrazeMOST POINTS WINS

Coconuts fall from three palm trees. Walk over one to pick it up, then carry it to your conveyor belt: red's belt is on the left, blue's is on the right.

  • 🥥 Coconut: 1 point for you.
  • 🧊 Ice coconut: freezes the other player for 3 seconds.
  • ➖ Negative coconut: takes 10 points from the other player (scores never go below 0).
  • 🤚 Snatch! Walk into the other player with empty hands to steal what they're carrying.
